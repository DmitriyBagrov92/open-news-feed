# Meridian on the App Store — release notes for P11

What the app needs before TestFlight and review: the owner's accounts and decisions, the metadata,
the review notes, the privacy answers, and the risks worth settling first. Engineering notes live in
`ios/CLAUDE.md`; the privacy manifest in `App/Resources/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.

## Owner checklist (cannot be done from the repo)

1. **Apple Developer Program** membership (paid) — done: team `WK25TS8CXF` (`project.yml`).
2. **App Store Connect app record**: bundle id `info.meridi.app`, primary language English, SKU of
   your choice. The name "Meridian" may be taken — have a fallback ("Meridian News", "Meridian —
   the world by freshness").
3. **App Store Connect API key** — done: a team key with the Admin role (xcodebuild's cloud-managed
   distribution certificate is refused to lower roles). On the release Mac the `.p8` lives in
   `~/.appstoreconnect/private_keys/` and its ids in `~/.appstoreconnect/meridian.env`
   (`ASC_KEY_ID`, `ASC_ISSUER_ID`, `ASC_KEY_PATH`, `DEVELOPMENT_TEAM`) — never in the repo.
   `scripts/archive.sh` signs and uploads with it; `node scripts/asc.mjs [METHOD] [route]` calls the
   App Store Connect API (reads freely, writes only with `--write`).
4. **Server**: deploy the moderation work (P4, commit 1562c5f and later) to meridi.info and set on
   Railway `ADMIN_TOKEN` (a long secret), `SUPPORT_EMAIL`, optionally `MODERATION_WEBHOOK_URL`. The
   app's comments, report, block and delete rely on those endpoints; App Review will try them.
5. **Moderation commitment**: reports reviewed within 24 hours (`scripts/moderate.mjs`), as the
   community rules promise.
6. **Decisions** below: full-text reading, age rating, screenshots.

Then: `ios/scripts/archive.sh` — the build lands in TestFlight after processing (internal testing
needs no review). The API cannot create an app record: that one is made in App Store Connect (Apps ›
+ › New App: iOS, bundle id `info.meridi.app` once registered, SKU `meridian-ios`).

## Decisions to take before review

- **Full article text (Guideline 5.2).** The story view extracts and shows the publisher's full
  text (reader view), as the web does. App Review may treat reproducing third-party articles
  without permission as an intellectual-property problem. Options: (a) keep it and be ready to
  explain (links back to the source on every story, no ads, no paywall bypass — paywalled stories
  show the description only); (b) ship the app with the description, the key points and "Read at
  source" only (a small switch in the story view); (c) keep it for publishers that allow it. (b) is
  the safest first submission.
- **Age rating.** User-generated comments (moderated) and news that reports violence: expect 13+ or
  16+ in Apple's current scale; answer "User-generated content: yes" and "Infrequent/mild violence
  (news)".
- **Screenshots** (ready, review them): `scripts/appstore-screenshots.sh` writes seven screens per
  device to `ios/screenshots/appstore/{iphone,ipad}/` — Today with the brief, a story with its key
  points, Bubble Battle, Ahead, Your Feed, the conversation, the feed further down in dark — on the
  6.9" iPhone 17 Pro Max (1320×2868) and the 13" iPad Pro in landscape (2752×2064), status bar at
  9:41 of the day they are taken (simctl cannot set the iPad's date, so the newsroom follows the
  day). The world is the App Store newsroom (`Tests/Newsroom`, generated by `scripts/newsroom.mjs`):
  invented outlets on `.example` domains, invented stories with no real people or brands,
  public-domain / CC0 photographs from Wikimedia Commons (`Tests/Newsroom/CREDITS.md`). The brief,
  key points and forecasts are scripted in the shape the on-device model writes and pass the app's
  own checks (`NewsroomIntegrationTests`). Captions or device frames are optional — App Store
  Connect takes the plain captures.

## App Review notes (draft)

```
No account is needed. The app shows news from public RSS feeds and news APIs, newest first; every
story links to its source ("Read at source").

User-generated content (Guideline 1.2): readers comment anonymously under a random ID. Before the
first comment they must accept the community rules (https://meridi.info/terms). A server-side
filter refuses slurs, threats and link spam. Every comment has a menu to report it (with a reason;
it disappears for the reporter at once and is hidden for everyone after three reports) and to
block its author (their comments disappear; unblock in Settings › Blocked commenters). Readers can
delete their own comments. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; operators can hide, remove and ban.
To try it: open any story, scroll to Comments (or tap the comment button in the dock).

AI features run on the device with Apple Intelligence where available (the brief, key points, the
"Ahead" forecast — labelled SPECULATIVE / AI-GENERATED · NOT NEWS). Without Apple Intelligence the
app falls back to extractive summaries and the forecast button does not appear.

Translation uses Apple's Translation framework; language packs download only when the reader asks.
```

## Privacy answers (App Store Connect › App Privacy)

Matches the manifest: **no tracking**. Data collected, not linked to the user, for App
Functionality only: *User Content › Other User Content* (comments), *Identifiers › User ID* (the
random anonymous ID that signs comments, votes and reports; resettable in Settings), *Usage Data ›
Product Interaction* (votes and reports). Everything else — preferences, saved stories, the taste
profile, translations done on the device — stays on the device. Export compliance: the app uses
only standard HTTPS (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`).
